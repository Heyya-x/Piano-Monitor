# Piano Monitor

Piano Monitor is a simple piano practice companion for Mac and iOS.

It was originally created to make practicing with a digital piano more convenient: the Mac listens to the piano’s audio input, plays the sound through a selected output device, and keeps track of practice time automatically.

## Features

### 🎹 Mac

- Monitor audio from a connected piano or other input device
- Play the input audio through a selected output device
- Automatically detect and record piano practice sessions
- Keep daily, weekly, and monthly practice records
- Stay quietly in the menu bar while you practice
- Automatically stops monitoring when the input audio becomes inactive

### 📱 iOS

The companion iOS app lets you view practice data recorded by the Mac.

- Discover the Mac on your local network
- View practice history and statistics
- Check daily, weekly, and monthly practice time

Piano Monitor uses your Mac as the place where practice is recorded, while the iOS app provides a convenient way to review your progress.

### Why Piano Monitor?

I wanted a simple way to practice on a digital piano while using the Mac as an audio monitor and a lightweight practice tracker.

Instead of manually starting and stopping a timer, Piano Monitor simply runs in the background and records practice sessions as you play.
